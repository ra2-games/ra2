# Allied air assault and ground fallback

Use when the observed sidebar offers Allied Rocketeers and water or chokepoints make air movement useful. These are original RA2 type names, not guaranteed availability in YR or mods. Choose each action from the current battle; this is not a build script or a fixed opening.

## Build an affordable force

Establish power (`GAPOWR`), infantry production (`GAPILE`) and income (`GAREFN`) under a real home guard. Against early American paradrops, start a small dog/GI batch as soon as the barracks is placed, alongside refinery construction; do not wait until attackers are already destroying production. Secure the next income investment before enlarging the assault queue; check current sidebar availability and confirmed placement rather than remembering a prerequisite as complete.

With the air-force prerequisite available (America: `AMRADR`), a small `JUMPJET` group can scout and support defense. Before enlarging it into the main assault, establish a visible target and approach it can survive. An unexplored enemy base is not evidence of weak anti-air. If the observed approach is defended, spend on a force that can address that obstacle instead of filling another air queue. Reserve recovery money and retain mobile anti-armor and anti-infantry cover at home. Keep a useful air reserve over production when visible raids threaten it; sending every new Rocketeer toward scouting can leave the base vulnerable before an assault exists. New units first replace necessary guard losses; the remainder joins a staging group.

Use one scout to find a route and targets. Fly through observable waypoints, checking actual displacement. Do not repeatedly change destinations before arrival or copy coordinates from another game.

## Decide whether to continue the air attack

| Current evidence                                                                                      | Next decision                                                                                                               |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Group remains healthy and the assigned objective is being cleared                                     | Continue the mission; reinforce at a safe staging point.                                                                    |
| Own units take repeated damage or confirmed losses near visible anti-air while the objective persists | Pull survivors out along the known approach. Inspect the obstacle before buying another assault batch.                      |
| An exposed approach avoids the observed defense                                                       | Scout it first, regroup, then commit the assembled force.                                                                   |
| Repeated approaches lose air groups to the same defended core                                         | Consider a ground route and siege force if the economy and sidebar support it. Do not repeat the failed air purchase cycle. |

Only infer losses from sufficient observations, not `leftView` alone. Do not assume hidden enemy health or an unknown cooldown. Use visible effects, own health and repeated views of the objective. A camera change does not cancel a productive native attack.

Changing Rocketeers to Harriers (`ORCA`) is not by itself evidence of a solution to anti-air. Before investing, explain how the observed approach and target can be attacked with the available controls. The current unit-order API requires the acting unit and attack target in the viewport at execution; do not assume off-screen selection survives camera changes. Repeated view/command failures call for a different actionable plan, not bypassing visibility checks.

## Ground fallback when justified

Confirm a traversable bridge/land route with a visible scout. Confirm the factory (`GAWEAP`), required tech and current sidebar entry before reserving a siege purchase such as `SREF`. A completed factory awaiting placement is still a blocker: resolve placement or choose a different feasible plan.

Keep tank/infantry cover with fragile siege units, and retain the home guard. Gather a usable group outside known enemy fire, then attack the exposed defense from its reachable approach. Verify movement and target removal before pushing deeper. Choose ground siege only when the observed route, defenses and economy support it.

## Stop rebuilding and finish

Deal with immediate threats needed for survival, then attack the construction yard and production chain. An imminent superweapon can justify a diversion, but repeated reconstruction calls for finding and attacking its source. Avoid spending the assault on distant support buildings while the core replaces losses.

After core production is gone, inspect remembered structures and explore the coast for surviving shipyards. Keep a short list of searched sectors and unresolved last-seen structures; finish those checks before repeating a broad sweep. Assign distinct search sectors to independently usable groups. Split surviving groups only when each can safely finish its objective; two survivors do not need identical repeated orders. Let search movements reach their destinations. If a final target persists, compare attacker displacement and visible firing across observations before changing the approach. A target still standing does not by itself prove a stuck attack. Reinforce a reachable target when the current force is too small; avoid repeatedly issuing the same attack without new evidence.

Stop writes on native end. Preserve the native banner or accessible result evidence; score alone and the score-page ID do not establish victory. Never restart to hide a loss or incomplete run.
